# Privacy Policy for YuPillTime

**Last updated:** September 7, 2026

YuStudio ("we", "our", or "us") operates the **YuPillTime** mobile application (the "Service"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service.

---

## 1. Information Collection and Use

**YuPillTime is designed as an offline-first, standalone utility.** We respect your privacy and do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal or medical information.

- **No Medical Data Collection:** Medication names, dosages, schedules, reminder logs, and notes entered into the application are stored **locally on your device** only. We do not operate an external database or backend server to sync, process, or access your medical information.
- **No Personal Account Required:** You do not need to register, log in, or provide personal identifiers (such as name, phone number, or email address) to use the application.

---

## 2. Device Permissions

To deliver core reminder functionalities, the application may request the following permissions:

- **Notifications (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`):** Used strictly to send medication reminder alerts to your device at your scheduled times.
- **Exact Alarms (`SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM` / `USE_EXACT_ALARM`):** Used solely to schedule high-precision local alarms so that medication reminders trigger accurately.

These permissions are utilized entirely within your local device system and are not used to collect telemetry or monitor user behavior.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services & Tracking

- The application does not integrate third-party advertising SDKs, behavioral tracking, or analytics tools.
- All reminder calculations and local notifications are processed entirely on-device.

---

## 4. Data Retention and Deletion

Because all application data resides strictly within your device's internal storage:
- You retain complete control over your data at all times.
- You can permanently remove all stored reminders and settings at any time by clearing the application data in your Android system settings or by uninstalling the application.

---

## 5. Children's Privacy

Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

---

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted directly on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
