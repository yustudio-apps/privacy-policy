數學闖關王 隱私權政策

最後更新：2026 年 10 月 28 日

數學闖關王（以下稱「本 App」）是給國小學童練習數學的 App，由 YuStudio 開發。我們重視孩子和家長的隱私。

一、我們不收集可以識別個人的資料

本 App 不需要註冊帳號，不收集姓名、電子郵件、電話、位置、裝置識別碼或任何可以識別個人的資料，也不使用廣告工具。

二、資料只存在您的手機裡

孩子的暱稱、考試紀錄、點數、獎勵設定、爸媽密碼（只存加密後的雜湊值）、自訂頭像照片與獎勵照片都存在這支手機的 App 裡，本 App 不會上傳到任何伺服器。

如果手機開啟了 Android 系統備份，系統可能把本 App 的資料備份到您自己的 Google 帳號；這是 Android 系統的功能，本 App 的開發者無法取得這些資料。

本 App 每天會把備份檔存到手機的「下載／數學闖關王」資料夾，備份檔由您自己保管；照片不在備份檔裡。家長也可以在爸媽專區用「分享」把備份檔傳到自己選擇的地方（例如自己的雲端硬碟），傳送與保管都由您自己決定，本 App 的開發者收不到。

三、應用程式內購買

「完整版」的購買由 Google Play 處理。本 App 只會向手機上的 Google Play 商店確認是否已購買，不會取得您的付款資料。

本 App 使用 Google 提供的 Google Play 付款元件（Play Billing Library），這個元件可能會依 Google 的政策把付款功能的診斷資訊傳送給 Google；這些資料由 Google 處理，本 App 的開發者收不到。詳情請參考 Google 的隱私權政策（https://policies.google.com/privacy）。

四、評分

家長在爸媽專區兌換獎勵後，本 App 可能透過 Google Play 的應用程式內評論功能邀請您評分；評分由 Google Play 處理。

五、兒童隱私

本 App 的使用對象包含 13 歲以下的兒童，遵守 Google Play 家庭政策：不放廣告，本 App 不收集兒童的個人資料；購買與評分只在需要爸媽密碼的爸媽專區出現。

六、刪除資料

移除本 App 或在系統設定清除本 App 的資料，就會刪除 App 裡的所有資料；「下載／數學闖關王」資料夾裡的備份檔請自行刪除。

七、聯絡我們

如有任何問題，請來信：yustudiolive@gmail.com

MathDress Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

MathDress (the "App") is a math practice app for elementary school children, developed by YuStudio. We care about the privacy of children and parents.

1. We do not collect personally identifiable information

The App does not require an account. It does not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, location, device identifiers, or any other information that can identify a person, and it does not use advertising tools.

2. Your data stays on your phone

Your child's nickname, test history, points, reward settings, the parent PIN (stored only as an encrypted hash), and custom avatar and reward photos are all stored in the App on this phone. The App does not upload them to any server.

If Android system backup is turned on, the system may back up the App's data to your own Google account. This is an Android system feature, and the App's developer cannot access that data.

Every day the App saves a backup file to the "Download/數學闖關王" folder on the phone. You keep and manage the backup files; photos are not included. Parents can also use "Export / share" in the Parent Zone to send a backup file to a place they choose (for example, their own cloud drive). Sending and storing it is entirely up to you, and the App's developer does not receive it.

3. In-app purchase

The "full version" purchase is handled by Google Play. The App only asks the Google Play Store on the phone whether it has been purchased, and never receives your payment information.

The App uses Google's Google Play payment component (Play Billing Library). This component may send diagnostic information about the payment feature to Google under Google's policies. That data is handled by Google, and the App's developer does not receive it. For details, see Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

4. Ratings

After a parent redeems a reward in the Parent Zone, the App may invite you to rate it using Google Play's in-app review feature. Ratings are handled by Google Play.

5. Children's privacy

The App's audience includes children under 13, and it follows the Google Play Families Policy: there are no ads, and the App does not collect children's personal information. Purchases and ratings appear only in the Parent Zone, which requires the parent PIN.

6. Deleting data

Uninstalling the App, or clearing its data in system settings, deletes all data in the App. Please delete the backup files in the "Download/數學闖關王" folder yourself.

7. Contact us

If you have any questions, please email: yustudiolive@gmail.com
